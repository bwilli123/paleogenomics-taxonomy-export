Phenotypic variant absence in the Ranis/Zlatý kůň genomes: no lactose tolerance, no light pigmentation, no light hair — traits that would later become common in European populations were absent in this earliest-sampled lineage.

The Lincombian–Ranisian–Jerzmanowician (LRJ) technocomplex at Ranis is now definitively attributed to *Homo sapiens* (not Neanderthal) via mitochondrial DNA from 11 bone fragments (Mylopotamitaki) — resolving a long-standing species-attribution debate for this stone-tool industry.

Zlatý kůň lacks a direct tool association but its kinship to Ranis 12 and proximity to the Nad Kačákem site make it a "plausible" LRJ maker — the technocomplex-debate gap remains open for sites lacking genomic data more broadly.

Rougier's site curation: the Ranis individuals occupied Ilsenhöhle only briefly, consistent with mobile rather than sedentary hunter-gatherer life.
