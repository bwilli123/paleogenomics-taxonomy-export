## About this export

This is a curated, read-only subtree of a larger private research taxonomy (17 domains, 1,000+ nodes, human-reviewed and cross-corpus-verified). Published as a standalone, versioned artifact — no accounts, no merge, no ongoing governance. One internal cross-reference (to an unrelated, unconfirmed interpretive framing in a different domain of the private taxonomy) was removed from this export as out of scope.

Sourcing and methodology: this section is cross-confirmed across 5 independently-synthesized source documents within a single processing batch, which is unusually high within-batch corroboration for a first-pass addition. It is currently single-notebook in origin; flagged internally for further cross-corpus confirmation if independent sources surface additional paleogenomic material in the future.
